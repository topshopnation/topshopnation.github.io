Everything Connected

Banks, credit cards, and brokerage accounts pulled into one place so your real net worth is a single glance instead of five different logins.

A Real Running Balance

Your balance after every transaction, not a once-a-day estimate. It works the way a paper register used to, just kept current automatically.

On Your Device

Your account data lives on your iPhone and in your own iCloud. There's no separate server storing your numbers.

No Budget to Learn

Nothing to configure and no system to adopt. Open it, see where you stand, close it.

What it's for

Sprig came out of the same annoyance a lot of people have: money is spread across a checking account, a couple of cards, a brokerage, maybe a 401(k), and none of it talks to the others. Getting an honest answer to "where do I actually stand right now" means opening four or five apps and doing the math yourself. Sprig's whole job is to skip that step, pull the accounts together, and show the number.

Messy transaction names are cleaned up as they come in, so a statement full of cryptic merchant codes turns into something you can actually read at a glance. That part happens automatically and gets better the more it sees.

Where your data goes

This one mattered enough to build around: account data is kept on the device and synced through the user's own iCloud, not sent off to sit on a company's server. Sprig isn't in the business of seeing anyone's financial data, so it's built so it doesn't have to.

Sprig is still being built and isn't in the App Store yet. The current state of the app, and what's coming, lives at its own site.
